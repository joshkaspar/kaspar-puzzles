HOW TO PLAY

BUILD A SYNTH, ONE PART AT A TIME
  1. Place. Each turn, pick one of the three parts in the bin and put it in any empty slot.
  2. Build. When parts match one of today's blueprints (turned or flipped any way), they glow. Tap a glowing part to choose where the module goes, then press BUILD. The parts clear, and the module takes that one slot. Building is optional.
  3. Patch. The red cable runs from OSC to OUT. Black modules built on the cable are patched and score by their rule. A raw part on the cable blocks it until you build over it.
  4. Knobs and utilities. Aluminum modulators and terracotta utilities can't go on the cable. They score from where they sit; LFOs and Envelopes add points to patched modules they touch.
  5. End. The patch ends when you can't place a part or build anything. Each empty slot and leftover part costs 1 point. Fill every cable slot for the complete-patch bonus, then play what you built. An open patch still plays: an empty cable slot sounds thin, and a part left on the cable crackles.

A new patch every day, the same for everyone. Progress is saved in this browser.
