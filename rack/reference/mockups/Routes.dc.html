CABLE ROUTES
Schematic sketches, not final art. Each day places two fixed jacks and a pre-laid cable between them. At the start of a game the whole cable lies loose in the case. Counts: cable cells · cells touching the cable · cells two or more steps away.
STRAIGHT ACROSS
Cable 3 · touching 6 · far 14
Only 3 cells, under the 4–6 target. Could be a tutorial day or an easy tier.
ONE STEP
Cable 4 · touching 6 · far 13
A single jog. Gentle, good for early days.
CORNER
Cable 5 · touching 9 · far 9
Jacks on neighbouring edges. Gives the most cells touching the cable.
S-CURVE
Cable 5 · touching 8 · far 10
Two bends; the middle run cuts across the board.
HOOK
Cable 5 · touching 8 · far 10
Both jacks on one edge. The U-turn leaves a pocket inside the bend.
LONG RUN
Cable 6 · touching 9 · far 8
6 cells from a corner jack. The busiest cable, with the fewest far cells.
BOXED-IN OUT
Cable 5 · touching 6 · far 12
OUT sits mid-board and the cable hugs the top edge. Hardest: the end of the cable is crowded.
KEY
Fixed jack (OSC or OUT)
Cable cell: shapers go here
Touching the cable: modulator zone
Far from the cable: utility zone
CABLE STATES
Loose in the case: not built yet
Patched: glossy, plugged at both ends
Blocked: a raw part sits on it
